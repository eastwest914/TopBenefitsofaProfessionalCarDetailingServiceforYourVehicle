What is a Professional Car Detailing Service?

Unlike a standard car wash, a car detailing service goes beyond surface cleaning. It involves a meticulous, step-by-step process to restore and protect every inch of your vehicle, from deep-cleaning carpets and seats to polishing the paintwork for a showroom-quality shine. At EM NJ Ceramic Coating, our detailing services include: Exterior Detailing – Paint correction, polishing, waxing, ceramic coating, wheel cleaning, and tire shine. Paint Protection Services – Application of ceramic coatings for long-lasting shine and durability. By investing in a professional detailing service, you protect your car from wear and tear, increase resale value, and enjoy that new-car feeling every time you get behind the wheel.
